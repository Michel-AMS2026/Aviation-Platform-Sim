Stylised network. 5 hubs (FRA, AMS, CDG, MAD, MUC), 48 European spokes, 18 long-haul destinations. 4 NWCs anchored to their actual hubs and alliances; 3 LCCs (Ryanair-, easyJet-, Vueling-style); 2 regional carriers feeding alliance hubs. Strict viability rule: an OD pair is counted as viable only when both legs are operated by carriers on the platform. Long-haul routes are operated only by NWCs. Route inventories are stylised patterns reflecting documented carrier archetypes; individual routes were not validated against published schedules.
